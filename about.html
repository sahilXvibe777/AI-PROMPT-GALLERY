About Us

PROMPT MATRIX is a free, fast and focused library of AI image prompts.

About Prompt Matrix

PROMPT MATRIX collects carefully written prompts for AI image generation, organised by style so you can find the right look quickly: GTA-style scenes, cyberpunk, anime, 3D, nature, cars, fashion, space and more.

Our Mission

To help creators, students and designers get great results from AI image tools without wasting time on trial and error, by giving them clear, descriptive prompts they can copy and customise.
